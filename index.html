Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common questions about 2FA, security, and offline usage.

Is this tool safe to use?
Yes. The 2FA Live tool operates on a zero-knowledge, client-side architecture. When you paste your secret key, it never leaves your browser. All cryptographic operations to generate the 2FA tokens are handled entirely by your local device.
Can I use this offline?
Absolutely. Once the web page is fully loaded, you can disconnect from the internet. The TOTP generator relies on your device's system clock and local Javascript, not an active internet connection, making it an incredibly secure authenticator online.
Why is my code being rejected?
Because TOTP codes are inherently time-based, your device's clock must be accurately synchronized. If your computer or phone's clock is off by even a minute, the generated 2FA code will be invalid. Please ensure your system time is set to sync automatically via the internet. Use our "Code Verifier & Drift Check" tool above to test past and future windows!
What secret key formats and services are supported?
We support standard Base32 keys (with or without spaces or dashes, case-insensitive) as well as standard otpauth://totp/... setup URLs. Works seamlessly with Google, GitHub, Facebook, Binance, Amazon, Microsoft, Discord, Twitter/X, and any RFC 6238 compliant service.
How do I backup my 2FA accounts safely?
Store your original Base32 secret keys or setup QR code images offline in an encrypted password manager (such as Bitwarden, KeePass, or 1Password) or an offline USB drive. Never save unencrypted plaintext keys on cloud notes or email drafts.
What is the difference between TOTP and HOTP?
TOTP (RFC 6238) is time-based, refreshing codes every 30 seconds according to the Unix epoch clock. HOTP (RFC 4226) is counter-based, advancing to the next code only when a login request is made. TOTP is universally preferred by modern services.
Is my secret key stored on any server or cloud?
Never. The application operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge paradigm. All calculations are executed in memory inside your browser. No analytics, tracking pixels, or database connections exist on the backend.
Why does TOTP use a 30-second time step?
RFC 6238 recommends 30 seconds as the optimal equilibrium between user usability (giving you sufficient time to read and type 6 digits) and security (rendering any intercepted code expired and useless almost immediately).
Can I use this as a desktop alternative to mobile authenticators?
Yes! Any account configured with standard Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy can be used directly here by providing its setup key or QR code.
What should I do if my system clock is out of sync?
Enable "Set time automatically" in your operating system date & time settings, or use our "Code Verifier & Drift Check" tool above to diagnose your clock offset.
